## 1. ICP, jobs to be done, trigger events

**Primary ICP: the one-person development shop.** Development director or executive director at a Jewish community organization with 100 to 2,000 donor households and no prospect-research budget: synagogues (250 to 800 families), day schools, yeshivas and seminaries, Chabad houses, JCCs, chesed organizations, Israel-based institutions raising in North America. Buys with a credit card up to about $2K/year without a board vote.

**Secondary ICP: the umbrella.** Federations, community foundations and day-school networks that run capacity-building programs for their agencies. They do not draft letters themselves; they sponsor tools for 10 to 60 agencies at once (Enterprise, multi-org).

**Tertiary: any mission-driven nonprofit** with the same one-person shape (onboarding already offers hospital, advocacy and "other nonprofit").

**Jobs to be done**
1. "Get the High Holiday / year-end appeal out to 300 people, personalized, without a week of copy-paste."
2. "Walk into a major-gift meeting knowing why this donor cares and what number to say."
3. "Stop letting warm donors go cold because nobody owns the follow-up."
4. "Give the board a pipeline number they believe."
5. "Keep donor data somewhere safer than the shared spreadsheet, without an IT project."

**Trigger events** (when they search or say yes)
- Elul (4 to 6 weeks before Rosh Hashanah) and the 6 weeks before Dec 31: appeal season.
- Post-chagim planning (Oct 5 onward) and post-year-end debrief (January): "we need a system."
- A capital campaign kickoff, a new building, a new ED or development director in the first 90 days.
- A federation allocation cut or a matching-grant deadline that forces individual-donor cultivation.
- A data scare: lost spreadsheet, a volunteer with the whole list on a personal laptop.

## 2. Positioning and messaging hierarchy

**One sentence:** ChaiRaise is the AI fundraising copilot and donor CRM for nonprofits and Jewish community organizations: it drafts personalized donor letters, appeals and outreach from your own donor records, suggests the ask, and tracks every relationship to a committed gift.

**Pillars**
1. **Written for you, from your data.** Six templates, batch appeals for 50 donors at once, donor briefs, outreach strategy, all from the org profile and the donor record. Proof: sample outputs on the homepage; the `/api/ai` route and template ladder in the repo.
2. **Knows Jewish giving.** Chai-aligned asks, High Holiday and year-end windows, yahrzeit fields, Shabbat send guard. Proof: `CHAI_ASK_LADDER`, calendar guidance, `isShabbat()` guard.
3. **Your donors stay yours.** Tenant isolation, own-mailbox sending, export and hard delete, AI that does not train on your data. Proof: `/security`, `lib/authz.js`, AES-256-GCM credential storage.

**Message ladder**
- Headline (outcome): "Donor letters, appeals and outreach, drafted in minutes, not afternoons."
- Sub (who + how): development directors and EDs; import donors, get the letter, the fit score, the ask, the pipeline.
- Proof: verifiable counts (6 / 10 / 50 / 100), founder origin (built inside a live campaign), security page.
- Offer: free for 100 donors, no card; Pro $149/mo billed annually with a 14-day trial.

**What we do not say:** that ChaiRaise writes grant proposals (it does not), any "18x" or "30-second" claims, anonymous testimonials, competitor feature claims we cannot verify.

**Freemium to paid ladder (in-product triggers already exist via `PlanGate`)**

3. Trial → annual Pro. Conversion nudges: day 3 "your appeal batch is ready", day 10 "trial ends in 4 days, here is what you generated".
4. Pro org inside a federation → introduce the federation to Enterprise multi-org.

**Pricing experiments to run (human decision, no code change needed for the first two)**

- "Appeal-season pass": 60-day Pro at a flat price sold in Elul and in November. Test in Stripe with a second price id.
- Enterprise anchor: $99 per agency per month for a federation buying 10+ seats, billed annually.

## 6. Growth loops and retention

- **Sample-output loop.** Every blog post and every LinkedIn post shows one generated paragraph with a fictional donor; readers try the free tier to see their own. Measure: `#samples` scroll depth (via `cr:cta` event `hero_samples`) and signups with `source=chairaise-home`.
- **Appeal-season loop.** Batch campaigns are Pro; appeal season forces the upgrade decision twice a year (Elul, November). Email the free base 3 weeks before each window.
- **Board loop.** The exported pipeline and audit trail end up in board packets; board members sit on other boards. Add a one-line footer to the export ("Generated with ChaiRaise") once a human approves.
- **Umbrella loop.** One agency in a federation on Pro → the federation hears about it at the professionals' meeting → Enterprise conversation.
- **Content loop.** Webinar Q&A → blog post → newsletter → next webinar.

Retention: weekly "3 donors going cold" email (data already exists in the priority leaderboard), the Jewish-calendar reminders (High Holidays, year-end, yahrzeits), and the fact that the donor list lives here. Watch the 30-day return rate of orgs that imported ≥ 20 donors versus those that did not; the CSV import is the retention event.

## 8. KPIs and weekly cadence

Source of truth: the `events` table and `/admin` (signup, org_created, donor_added, ai_used, email_sent, checkout_started, subscribed) plus Stripe.

Weekly (Monday, 30 minutes): signups; activation rate (org_created → ai_used within 7 days; target 50%); letters generated per active org (target 5/week); trial starts; trial-to-paid (target 20%); MRR and churn; top 3 blog pages by impressions in Search Console; newsletter subscribers from `chairaise-home`.

Monthly: CAC by channel (founder time counted at a nominal rate), NRR, partner pipeline (conversations, workshops booked, agencies covered), ranking positions for the 12 queries in §4a.

## 9. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| "AI grant writing" traffic bounces because we do not write grants | The FAQ and the explainer post say so in the first sentence; capture the visitor with the org-profile output (mission summary, programs, talking points), which is the start of a grant narrative; track the request count before building it. |
| Annual-only Pro is too big a first commitment for a small shul | Test monthly and the appeal-season pass (§3); keep Starter genuinely useful. |
| AI output quality varies with thin donor records | The letter template ladder falls back to T-E for thin records; onboarding should push CSV import with focus areas and prior gift fields; show a "record completeness" nudge (already an engagement-score input). |
| Donor-data trust objection from boards | `/security` is already itemized; add a one-page PDF version; offer a data-processing addendum on request. |
| Seasonality: dead weeks around chagim and in July | Launch post-chagim; sell the appeal-season pass in Elul and November; use summer for partner and content work. |
| Founder time is the bottleneck | Ship the 12 posts and 5 blog drafts before T0; every webinar is recorded and reused. |
| Shared Stripe account across products | `isChaiRaiseSubscription()` already guards the webhook; keep the `chairaise_plan` metadata on every checkout. |
