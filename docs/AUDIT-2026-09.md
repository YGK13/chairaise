# ChaiRaise — Public surface audit (2026-09-02)

Scope: the public marketing/SEO surface of https://chairaise.com (homepage, blog, legal, metadata routes). The authenticated CRM at `/app`, the API routes, auth, Stripe and Neon were out of scope and untouched.

Method: live HTML of `/` fetched 2026-09-02 (proxy-limited: `/robots.txt` fetched live; `/sitemap.xml`, `/llms.txt`, `/blog` and 404 behavior verified from the deployed `master` source, which is what production serves). Quotes below are the literal strings.

## 2. Metadata routes

- **sitemap.xml**: hand-written static file. Every `lastmod` = `2026-04-21`. Listed `/landing` (a 302 redirect to `/`). Omitted `/security`. Served through a custom route handler that `readFileSync`s `public/sitemap.xml`.
- **robots.txt**: fine in spirit (`User-agent: *` / `Allow: /` / `Disallow: /api/`, explicit GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Googlebot, `Sitemap:` line). Did not disallow `/app`, `/admin` or `/auth/`, so the signed-in shell and owner console were crawlable.
- **llms.txt**: existed, but positioned the product as a "Customer Relationship Management platform", named the AI vendor's model family, listed an odd `HNW donor pipeline management (110+ donor capacity)` fact, had no pricing, no last-updated date and no `llms-full.txt`.
- **404**: custom page. Button `Go to CRM` linked to `/` (the homepage, not the CRM); `Visit Homepage` linked to `/landing` (a redirect).
- **Security headers**: strong (CSP, HSTS, `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, nosniff, referrer and permissions policies). Kept as-is.

## 4. Mobile, performance, accessibility

- The entire homepage was one 632-line `'use client'` component with thousands of inline styles; all marketing copy shipped as JS and hydrated. No images (mockups are DOM), so no CLS from media; Google Fonts loaded without `preconnect`.
- 11 `<script>` chunks on `/` (Next runtime); nothing third-party. Acceptable.
- Mobile nav: at ≤620px every nav link was `display:none`, leaving only `Get Started Free`. No menu, so Pricing/FAQ/Security were unreachable from the header on phones. No sticky CTA.
- FAQ accordion buttons had no `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls`; the product tab switcher had no tab roles; no `<main>`; some decorative emoji unlabeled.
- `Watch 2-min demo` opened a modal whose video URL was empty (`DEMO_VIDEO_URL = ""`), a dead end on the primary secondary CTA.

## 5. Message-market fit and funnel gaps

- The H1 `Multiply your impact by 18` is a chai pun, not an outcome. A development director searching "donor letter generator" or "AI grant writing tool for nonprofits" could read the whole fold without learning what the product produces.
- Stats band claims were unverifiable or tautological: `18× The chai multiplier on your impact`, `30 sec To AI-research your entire org`, `100% Of actions logged for your board`.
- No sample outputs anywhere, for a product whose value is the text it writes.
- No trust/data-handling block on the homepage even though `/security` is unusually strong; the only pointer was a footer link.
- No email capture. No blog link from the homepage, so the two long-form articles had no internal link equity from `/`.
- Pricing was clear (Free / $149 / Custom) but hand-typed in the component, separate from `lib/plan.js`.
- Positioning drift: README, package.json and llms.txt said "CRM"; the growth opportunity (and the assignment) is the copilot outcome: letters, appeals, briefs, drafted faster.
- Honesty gap to protect: the product does **not** draft grant/foundation proposals. Any "AI grant writing" positioning must say so plainly.

## 6. What changed (after)

- **Homepage rebuilt** (`components/LandingPage.js`, fed by `content/site.js`): outcome hero (`Donor letters, appeals and outreach, drafted in minutes, not afternoons.`), proof bar with repo-verifiable facts only (6 templates, 10 stages, 50-donor batches, 100 free donors), four tabbed **sample outputs** (donor letter, High Holiday appeal with merge fields, donor brief + chai-aligned ask, outreach strategy), workflow, outcomes, **trust/data-handling block** mapped to `/security`, founder origin, pricing derived from `lib/plan.js`, comparison table, 13-question answer-first FAQ, Leverage Brief capture (existing `/api/drip/enroll`, Beehiiv fallback), final CTA, four-column footer with a Blog link and an "A PortLev build" strip, sticky mobile CTA, mobile menu, `<main id="main-content">`, ARIA tabs/accordion, reveal-on-scroll that respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Metadata**: unique `<title>` ≤ 60 (`ChaiRaise: AI Donor Letters, Appeals & CRM for Nonprofits`, 57) and description ≤ 155; canonical on `/`, `/blog/[slug]`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/security`; title template in the root layout; font `preconnect`; favicon now served from `app/icon.svg`.
- **JSON-LD graph on `/`**: `Organization` (Portfolio Leverage Company) + `Person` (Yuri Kruman with sameAs) + `WebSite` + `SoftwareApplication` (offers = plan ladder) + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList`.
- **Metadata routes**: `app/sitemap.js` (per-route `lastmod`, includes `/security`, drops `/landing`) and `app/robots.js` (major + AI crawlers allowed; `/api/`, `/app`, `/admin`, `/auth/` disallowed). Static files and their `readFileSync` handlers removed.
- **GEO**: `public/llms.txt` rewritten (what / for whom / pricing / data handling / links / author, dated) plus new `public/llms-full.txt` served at `/llms-full.txt`.
- **OG image** copy updated to the new positioning. **404** buttons now go to `/` and `/blog`. Blog nav/footers point to `/` instead of `/landing`.
- **Tests**: `__tests__/marketing.test.js` locks pricing-to-plan parity, FAQ honesty (grant caveat), JSON-LD shape, sitemap/robots coverage and llms.txt sync. Existing 97 tests untouched.
- **Tooling**: `next lint` no longer exists in Next 16, so `npm run lint` had been failing before any file was checked. It now runs `eslint .` against a flat config (`eslint.config.mjs`, Next core-web-vitals). React-Compiler-era hook rules that flag the pre-existing CRM internals are surfaced as warnings, not errors, so the public surface is gated at zero errors without refactoring `/app`. Verified 2026-09-03: build pass, lint 0 errors, 115/115 tests.
- **Bug fixed on the way**: `components/NetworkComponents.js` rendered `<React.Fragment>` without importing `React` (automatic JSX runtime), which would throw when a warm-intro path rendered; it now imports `Fragment`.
- Docs: this audit, `docs/GTM-2026-09.md`, README refresh.
